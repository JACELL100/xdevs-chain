# xDEVS Chain brand

## Concept

The mark is two chevrons from the THE xDEVS "X", a `>` and a `<`, drawn as hollow links and interlocked so that together they form the X. The `>` passes over the `<` at the top crossing and under it at the bottom, which is exactly how two chain links hold each other. It reads as code brackets, as a chain, and as the parent company's X, in the same brushed-silver, double-outlined style.

The wordmark is set in Geist (SIL OFL): "xDEVS" at weight 640, "Chain" at 420 in a lighter tone, outlined to paths, so no font is needed.

## Clear space and size

- Keep clear space around the symbol equal to **one quarter of its height** on every side (16 px around a 64 px mark). For lockups, measure from the symbol.
- Minimum sizes: hollow mark 24 px; below that use `xdevs-chain-mark-solid.svg` (works down to 16 px). Horizontal lockup at least 20 px tall.
- Don't recolor the metallic gradient, add effects, rotate, stretch, or separate the two links. Don't place the silver mark on light or busy backgrounds; use the ink or mono files instead.
