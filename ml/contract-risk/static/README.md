Demo of [xDEVS Contract Risk](https://huggingface.co/__MODEL__). The model (LightGBM trees + a linear model) runs in your
browser: paste a contract address on Ethereum or xDEVS Chain Enclave, or raw bytecode, and get a risk score with the
signals behind it. A score is advice, not proof.
